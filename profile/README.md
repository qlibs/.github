## Qlibs++ - C++ libraries

> ### Mission

- #### Correctness

  - Focuses on `composability`, `declarativeness` and `testability`
  - Incorporates `compile-time` self-testing upon `include/import` and/or exposes `runt-time` sanity-checks

- #### Performance

  - Applies `compile-time` and arch-driven `run-time` optimizations
  - Utilizes [fast to compile](https://qlibs.github.io/mp) `metaprogramming` techniques

- #### Simplicity

  - Strives for [minimal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unix_philosophy) `API`, `dependencies` and `ease of integration` (<i>single header/module</i>)
  - Supports experimenting on https://compiler-explorer.com

> [gcc](https://gcc.gnu.org), [clang](https://clang.llvm.org), [msvc](https://visualstudio.microsoft.com/vs/features/cplusplus)\*
