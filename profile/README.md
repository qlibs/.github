## Qlibs++ - C++ libraries

> ### Mission

- #### Correctness

  - Focuses on `composability`, `declarativeness`, `ease of testing`
  - `Compile-time` self-testing upon `import`*

- #### Performance

  - Applies `compile-time` and `arch` driven run-time optimizations
  - Strive for [`fast`](https://qlibs.github.io/mp) compilation-times

- #### Simplicity

  - Strive for [minimal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unix_philosophy) `API`, `dependencies`, `ease of integration`
  - Available on https://compiler-explorer.com

> Daily [releases](https://github.com/qlibs/qlibs/releases) ([gcc](https://gcc.gnu.org), [clang](https://clang.llvm.org), [msvc](https://visualstudio.microsoft.com/vs/features/cplusplus)\*)
